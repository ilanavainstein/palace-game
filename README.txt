RULES
1. each player draws three cards and puts them face down
2. each player draws six cards and chooses three to place face up on top of the face down cards
3. the top card is drawn from the deck

repeat for each round:
4. player chooses cards to play:
    - if card is not a power card, it must be placed on top of a card with less than or equal number (ace is highest)
    - player can play multiple cards at once if they are the same number
    - power cards can be played on top of anything:
        - 2 (reset): player can play anything on top of it
        - 10 (bomb): clears the deck, player can play anything
        - 7 (7 or below): next player must play a 7 or below
        - 9 (invisible): next player must play on top of the previous card
    - if the pile has 4 cards of the same number, it clears and the player who plays the last card goes again (functions as a 10)
    - if player can't play anything, choose to pick up the pile or use the top card of the draw pile; if it's not a valid card, pick up the pile plus this card
5. if there are cards left in the draw pile and player has less than 3 cards, draw a card

6. when the player uses all their cards and there are no cards left in the draw pile, use the three face-up cards
7. when the only cards left are the face-down cards, pick randomly
8. the player the finish all their cards first wins
